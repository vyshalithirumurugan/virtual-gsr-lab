GSR Laboratory

Galvanic Skin Resistance

Explore basal skin resistance and skin response during relaxation and mild stimuli.

Apparatus

GSR biofeedback unit, finger electrodes, input cable and stopwatch. The simulator uses generated values and does not measure a real subject.

Theory

GSR is the change in electrical resistance of the skin associated with sweat gland activity. During tension or arousal, sweat gland activity generally increases and skin resistance falls. During relaxation, resistance generally rises.

BSR: Basal skin resistance, the baseline or average resistance.

GSR: Phasic change in resistance due to a stimulus.

A small constant DC current is passed through the skin. Resistance follows Ohm's law: R = V/I.

Biofeedback is an educational process for self-regulation, not a treatment.
